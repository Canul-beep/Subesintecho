Datos Locales

Mexico: The Reality

13% of board seats in listed Mexican companies are held by women. (IMCO, 2024)

Women in CEO roles dropped from 4% to 3% between 2023 and 2024. (IMCO, 2024)

Mexico ranks 3rd from the bottom in the OECD for female board representation.

At the current pace, Mexico will not achieve gender parity on corporate boards until 2052. (IMCO, 2024)

"Mexico will not have equal boards until 2052. That is not equality. That is not even equity."

— Sube Sin Techo, 2026

Theory & Framework

Understanding the Gap

01 / Structure

Gender as a Social Structure

Gender is not biology. It is built through cultural, political, and legal elements that assign different roles and access to resources to men and women.

León Tovar, 2020

02 / Power

Hegemonic Masculinity

The dominant, culturally exalted form of masculinity associated with authority and dominance. In business, this defines what 'leadership' is supposed to look like — and it was never designed with women in mind.

Connell & Messerschmidt, 2005

03 / Justice

Equality vs. Equity

Equality means giving everyone the same. Equity means giving each person what they need to reach the same outcome. Mexico achieved equality in politics but has not applied equity thinking to the private sector.

Key Framework

04 / Pipeline

The Broken Rung

In 2024, for every 100 men promoted to their first managerial position, only 81 women were promoted. This first missed step compounds at every level above it.

McKinsey & LeanIn.Org, 2024

Our Campaign

About Sube Sin Techo

Who We Are

A campaign created by business students at Tecnológico de Monterrey, built on academic research and course frameworks from Gender, Society and Human Rights.

Who It's For

University business students aged 18 to 25 who are about to enter the workforce and will shape the corporate cultures of tomorrow.

What We Use

The equity vs. equality framework, intersectionality, and gender theory to show students the structural gap they will face and position them as part of the solution.

Take Action

Be Part of the Change

Mentor Women in Your Program

Use your position to support, listen and open doors for women in your academic and professional circle.

Challenge Gender Bias

Speak up, question assumptions and make space for everyone's ideas in group projects and team environments.

Advocate for Equal Hiring

Push for fair opportunities and diverse teams in the companies and internships you join.

Being a future business leader means choosing what kind of culture you build.
